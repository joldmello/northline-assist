# Northline Mutual — Roadside Assistance Endorsement (Plus)
Policy form RSA-PLUS-2024. Applies to active personal auto policies with the Plus roadside endorsement.

## §1 Eligibility
Roadside benefits apply only while the policy is in force (status: Active). Lapsed, cancelled, or suspended policies have no roadside benefits. The operator of the vehicle at the time of the incident must be a listed driver on the policy. Unlisted drivers are not covered.

## §2 Covered events
Plus includes everything in Standard, plus collision towing:

### §2.1 Flat tire
Installation of the vehicle's spare, or a mobile technician if no spare is present.

### §2.2 Battery
Jump-start of a depleted 12-volt battery.

### §2.3 Lockout
Unlocking the insured vehicle.

### §2.4 Mechanical disablement (tow)
If the vehicle cannot be made safe to drive at the scene, towing to the nearest qualified repair facility, up to the mileage cap in §3.

### §2.5 Collision or overturn (tow only)
If the insured vehicle is disabled by a collision or overturn on a public road, Plus covers a tow to the nearest qualified body or mechanical shop. This is a roadside tow benefit, not a physical-damage settlement. Body repair, rental cars, and taxis are not dispatched by this endorsement.

## §3 Towing mileage cap
Plus plans include 50 miles of towing per disablement.

## §4 Exclusions
The following remain excluded under Plus:
- Vehicles used in racing, timed runs, drag events, or on a closed track.
- Unlisted drivers, excluded drivers, or operators under the influence.
- Vehicles used for hire, delivery, or commercial hauling.
- Body repair, parts, storage fees, and rental or taxi booking (those are claims or concierge services, not this endorsement).
- Lapsed or cancelled policies.

## §5 Customer communication
After a coverage determination, the member should receive a written summary of the next step (mobile unit, tow to a named shop, or referral to a claims specialist).
