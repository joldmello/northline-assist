# Northline Mutual — Lapsed policy notice (no roadside)
This file is attached to cancelled or lapsed policies. It is not an endorsement.

## §1 Status
The policy is not in force. No roadside, collision, or comprehensive benefits apply. Any request must be referred to a human specialist. Do not dispatch a truck.

## §2 Reinstatement
Coverage may resume only after premium is received and a new declarations page is issued. Roadside has a 48-hour waiting period after reinstatement.

## §3 Customer communication
Tell the caller the policy is not active and that a specialist will follow up. Do not imply a truck is on the way.
