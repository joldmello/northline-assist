# Northline Mutual — Roadside Assistance Endorsement (Standard)
Policy form RSA-STD-2024. Applies to active personal auto policies with the Standard roadside endorsement.

## §1 Eligibility
Roadside benefits apply only while the policy is in force (status: Active). Lapsed, cancelled, or suspended policies have no roadside benefits. The operator of the vehicle at the time of the incident must be a listed driver on the policy. Unlisted drivers, excluded drivers, and permit-only operators are not covered.

## §2 Covered events
The following are covered when the insured vehicle is disabled on a public road or public parking area in the United States:

### §2.1 Flat tire
Installation of the vehicle's spare, or a mobile technician if no spare is present. Purchasing a new tire is not covered.

### §2.2 Battery
Jump-start of a depleted 12-volt battery. Battery replacement is not covered.

### §2.3 Lockout
Unlocking the insured vehicle. Key cutting or key replacement is not covered.

### §2.4 Mechanical disablement (tow)
If the vehicle cannot be made safe to drive at the scene (engine failure, transmission, overheating), towing to the nearest qualified repair facility, up to the mileage cap in §3.

## §3 Towing mileage cap
Standard plans include 15 miles of towing per disablement. Distance beyond 15 miles is the customer's expense.

## §4 Exclusions
The following are not covered under Standard roadside:
- Collision, overturn, or any incident that is an auto physical-damage claim. Standard roadside does not dispatch for crash damage; the member must file a collision claim.
- Vehicles used in racing, timed runs, drag events, or on a closed track.
- Unlisted drivers, excluded drivers, or operators under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- Vehicles used for hire, delivery, rideshare while on trip, or commercial hauling.
- Disablement on unpaved private property more than 100 feet from a public road.
- Running out of fuel more than twice per policy year, routine maintenance, or aftermarket equipment failure.

## §5 Customer communication
After a coverage determination, the member should receive a written summary of the next step (mobile unit, tow, or referral to a claims specialist).
